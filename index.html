Connect the photo AI

The app uses Google's Gemini API to read your photos. The free tier costs nothing and needs no card. Your key is stored only on this device.

  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with a Google account.
  2. Tap Create API key and copy it.
  3. Paste it below.

You can skip this and add the key later in Settings. Without it, photo scanning will not work but manual logging will.
